IRON GRAVEL, a reference Gravel event for its beauty and hardness. It’s Gravel, yes, but it’s Iron. The steep terrain in the interior of the province of Alicante leaves no one indifferent and its 180 kms with 5,100+ make it a personal challenge that every gravel rider wants to have in its minority. Tracks, mountain roads and paths with 2 more shorter distance options, 110k and 55k, equally attractive to enjoy a day that you will hardly forget.
