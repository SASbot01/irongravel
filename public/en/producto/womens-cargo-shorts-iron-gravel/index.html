Women’s Cargo Shorts – Iron Gravel

Get ready for your next big adventure with our Gravel Cargo Shorts – the perfect cargo shorts. These shorts are designed to make your rides more comfortable and help you perform at your best. Made with high-quality materials and innovative features, they’re built to meet the most demanding standards.

Shorts Details:

  • Stay Cool and Dry: High-quality fabrics wick away moisture, keeping you comfortable even on the hottest days.
  • Ride Harder and Better: Advanced compression technology supports your muscles.
  • Extra Storage: Convenient pockets provide extra storage for all your essentials without getting in the way. It features easy-access pockets and two extra pockets on the back.
  • Unparalleled Comfort: Multi-panel construction and flatlock seams create a smooth fit, reducing chafing and increasing comfort.
  • Cushioned Support: High-density chamois padding provides excellent shock absorption, making long hours in the saddle much more comfortable.
  • Increased Visibility: Reflective details increase your visibility in low-light conditions, improving your safety.
  • Secure Fit: Laser-cut rubber bands keep the shorts in place without feeling tight, ensuring a secure fit.
  • UV Protection: Added UV protection protects your skin from the sun’s harmful rays, allowing you to ride safely in the sun.
  • Greater Storage Capacity Cargo: Extra storage capacity lets you carry everything you need on your rides.
