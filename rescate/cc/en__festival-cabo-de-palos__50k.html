CABO DE PALOS (CARTAGENA)
A picturesque fishing village with an iconic lighthouse, Cabo de Palos is known for its crystal-clear waters, ideal for diving and snorkelling, as well as its vibrant harbour and fresh seafood restaurants. The protected marine environment offers an incomparable spectacle of underwater life.

CALBLANQUE NATURAL PARK
This park is a paradise of pristine beaches, golden dunes and rugged mountains. Calblanque stands out for its biodiversity and undisturbed landscapes, being a perfect natural refuge for hikers and nature lovers. Its coast is wild and quiet, far from mass tourism.

THE MINES AND THE VILLAGE OF PORTMÁN
A place where the mining history of the region comes to life. Portmán, famous for its past in mineral extraction, is a testament to the rise and fall of the mining industry. The abandoned mines and the partially reclaimed bay are a reflection of its legacy.

LA MANGA DEL MAR MENOR
A stretch of land that separates the Mar Menor from the Mediterranean, La Manga is famous for its endless beaches and its sports offering, especially for water sports enthusiasts. With a salt lagoon of warm waters, it is an idyllic destination for sun and beach tourism.
